Please cite the following publications and repositories when using the `neville_mx_amplicon` pipeline or its components:

## Amplicon & Nanopore Methods

- **Multiplex Long-read Sequencing**:  
  Whitford, W., Hawkins, V., Moodley, K.S. et al. *Proof of concept for multiplex amplicon sequencing for mutation identification using the MinION nanopore sequencer.* Sci Rep 12, 8572 (2022). [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-12613-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-12613-7)

- **UMI Amplicon Sequencing**:  
  Karst, S.M., Ziels, R.M., Kirkegaard, R.H. et al. *High-accuracy long-read amplicon sequences using unique molecular identifiers with Nanopore or PacBio sequencing.* Nat Methods 18, 165–169 (2021). [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-020-01041-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-020-01041-y)

## Bioinformatics Tools

- **Dorado Basecaller & Aligner**:  
  Oxford Nanopore Technologies. *Dorado.* GitHub Repository: [https://github.com/nanoporetech/dorado](https://github.com/nanoporetech/dorado)

- **Filtlong Read Filtering**:  
  Wick, R. *Filtlong.* GitHub Repository: [https://github.com/rrwick/Filtlong](https://github.com/rrwick/Filtlong)

- **DeepSomatic Variant Calling**:  
  J. Park et al. *DeepSomatic: Accurate somatic small variant discovery for multiple sequencing technologies.* bioRxiv 2024.08.16.608331. [https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.08.16.608331](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.08.16.608331)

  Lai Z, Markovets A, Ahdesmaki M, et al. *VarDict: a novel and versatile variant caller for next-generation sequencing in cancer research.* Nucleic Acids Res 44(11):e108 (2016). [https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkw227](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkw227)

- **Sniffles2 Structural Variant Calling**:  
  Smolka, M., Paulin, L.F., Grochowski, C.M. et al. *Detection of mosaic and population-level structural variants with Sniffles2.* Nat Biotechnol 42, 1571–1580 (2024). [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-023-02024-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-023-02024-y)

- **Mosdepth Depth Computation**:  
  Pedersen BS, Quinlan AR. *Mosdepth: quick coverage calculation for genomes and exomes.* Bioinformatics 34(5):867-868 (2018). [https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btx699](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btx699)

- **VT Normalization**:  
  Adrian Tan, Gonçalo R. Abecasis and Hyun Min Kang. *Unified Representation of Genetic Variants.* Bioinformatics 31(13):2202-2204 (2015). [https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btv112](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btv112)

  McLaren, W., Gil, L., Hunt, S.E. et al. *The Ensembl Variant Effect Predictor.* Genome Biol 17, 122 (2016). [https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-016-0974-4](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-016-0974-4)

- **Sequali QC**:  
  Ruben H P Vorderman. *Sequali: efficient and comprehensive quality control of short- and long-read sequencing data.* Bioinformatics Advances 5(1):vbaf010 (2025). [https://doi.org/10.1093/bioadv/vbaf010](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioadv/vbaf010)

- **MultiQC compilation**:
